Testing: the road not taken

The obvious end-to-end test is to actually decode the QR code and assert on the URL. We didn't.

  • It needs a screenshot plus a decoder gem — adding a dependency, two slides after deleting one.
  • Slow, and brittle across the two browser drivers the suite runs under.
  • It tests the wrong failure. A maintained encoder does not quietly encode the wrong bytes.

The failure that actually happens is duller: the library emits a blank or empty code, or the view renders nothing at all. Test for that.
