Choosing a gem

  • rqrcode — pure Ruby, maintained, renders SVG. 2k stars, 241 forks. Chosen.
  • rqrcode_core — encoder only. rqrcode is the thin bit on top.
  • socketry/qrcode — too new, too few stars.
  • barby — a dozen symbologies. Far too much.
  • libqrencode bindings — C extension, build toolchain.

Fewest dependencies wins. And SVG, not PNG.
