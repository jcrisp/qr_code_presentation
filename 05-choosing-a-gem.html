Choosing a gem

  • rqrcode — pure Ruby, well maintained, renders SVG. Chosen.
  • rqrcode_core — the encoder underneath rqrcode. rqrcode is a thin renderer on top, so going lower buys nothing but writing the SVG myself.
  • barby — a general barcode toolkit covering a dozen symbologies. Far more surface than one QR needs, and its image output pulls in the same PNG library.
  • libqrencode bindings — a C extension. A build toolchain and one more thing to fail on deploy, for a job pure Ruby does fine.

The deciding rule: the most beautiful solution has the fewest dependencies. Pure Ruby, no native extension — and SVG output, which turns out to matter more than it looks.
